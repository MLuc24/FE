Call Script & Notes

Conversation Script - Course Inquiry

1. Opening (30 seconds)

"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] from EduCenter. Thank you for your interest in our English courses. Do you have a few minutes to discuss your learning goals?"

2. Qualification Questions (2-3 minutes)

  • "What's your current English level?"
  • "What's your main goal for improving English?"
  • "When would you like to start?"
  • "What's your preferred learning schedule?"

3. Course Recommendation (2-3 minutes)

"Based on what you've told me, I think our [Course Name] would be perfect for you because..."

4. Objection Handling

  • Price: "I understand budget is important. Let me show you the value you'll get..."
  • Time: "Our flexible schedule options can work around your busy lifestyle..."
  • Uncertainty: "That's perfectly normal. How about we start with a free trial class?"

5. Closing (1-2 minutes)

"Shall we schedule your placement test and trial class for this week?"
